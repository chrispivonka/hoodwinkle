How to play
  1. Read today's strange-but-true fact with the key word blanked out.
  2. Write a believable lie to fill the blank. Other players will see it.
  3. Pick the real answer out of the lies. Truth is worth 100 points.
  4. Every player who falls for your lie earns you 10 more, all day long.
